# Intake: fields to collect before drafting posts

Gather these before writing. Rapid response is fast, so pull what you can from the
user's request, ask only for what you truly need, and flag gaps rather than
stalling. Each field is annotated with what it drives.

## The source

- **Source item.** The news article, press release, or opponent post to respond
  to. Paste the text or the key facts. This is what the posts react to.
- **Key facts.** The specific facts to anchor the posts, so they stay accurate
  under speed.

## The angle

- **Campaign's take or angle.** The one point the campaign wants to make about the
  item. Shared across all four versions. Without it, ask before drafting.
- **Do-not-say.** Words, claims, or framings to avoid, including any from the
  candidate. Applied to every version.

## Channels and handles

- **Platforms in use.** Which of X, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, and short video
  the campaign posts to. Drives the platform variations.
- **Handles to tag and hashtags.** Accounts to mention and tags to include.
- **Link to include.** The article, release, or landing page the posts point to.
- **Donation link.** The contribution URL for the fundraising call-to-action
  version.

## Voice

- **Tone defaults.** Any baseline tone the campaign holds, recognizing that the
  professional, populist, and local versions each stretch it differently.
- **Timing.** How urgent this is, which affects how much polish versus speed to
  apply.

Confirm the facts before posting. Speed does not excuse an inaccurate claim.
