# Bridging techniques

Bridging is how a candidate moves from the question they were asked to the message
they want to land, without sounding evasive. Done well, it answers honestly and
then redirects. Done badly, it ignores the question and the room notices.

## The pattern: answer, bridge, message

1. **Answer.** Give a short, honest response to the actual question first. Skipping
   this is what makes a pivot read as a dodge.
2. **Bridge.** Use a transition phrase to move to the ground you want.
3. **Message.** Land on one of the campaign's core points, with a proof point if
   time allows.

Keep the answer brief and the message clear. The bridge is the hinge, not the
content.

## Bridge phrases

Transitions that move from question to message:

- "That matters, and here is what matters even more to families in this
  district."
- "I will answer that directly, and it connects to the bigger issue, which is..."
- "The real question voters are asking me is..."
- "Here is what I would actually do about it..."
- "That is exactly why I am running, because..."

Vary them. The same phrase repeated all night sounds rehearsed.

## Handling hostile questions and attacks

Use a block, bridge, message rhythm:

- **Block:** one calm sentence of truth that defuses the attack. Do not repeat the
  attack's language; restating it amplifies it.
- **Bridge:** transition to the issue voters care about.
- **Message:** land the campaign point and, if natural, a contrast.

Stay composed. The audience reads calm as strength and rattled as weakness, often
more than they hear the words.

## Do and do not

- Do answer the question first, even briefly.
- Do keep a short list of two or three points to bridge to, and use them.
- Do end on message and, in the close, on the ask.
- Do not repeat a false or hostile framing, even to deny it.
- Do not bridge so hard you never touch the question; that is the dodge voters
  punish.
- Do not take the bait on personal attacks; redirect to substance.

## Drill it

Bridging is a skill, not a script. Practice the same answer at 30 seconds and 2
minutes, and practice bridging from the most hostile version of each question, so
the pivot is automatic under pressure.
