# Intake: fields to collect before writing the pages

Gather these before writing. Pull what you can from the user's request, ask for
the essentials that are missing, and use clear placeholders for links and legal
text. Each field is annotated with what it drives.

## Where these fields come from

If a `campaigns/<name>.md` profile exists, reuse the shared fields from it and do not
re-ask. Run the `campaign-setup` skill to create one if there is none.

- From the profile: candidate name, office, district or jurisdiction, party,
  opponent(s), top issues, default tone, and key dates (election date and early-vote
  window). The profile's notes section may also hold do-not-say language, a slogan,
  or endorsements.
- Ask per task (not stored in the profile): the core message or slogan, the bio
  basics, the endorsements list, upcoming events, the volunteer needs, the donation
  link, the required disclaimer text, the contact details and social handles, the
  SEO keywords beyond name, office, and location, the first-priority audience, and
  which pages to build.

The field notes below still apply; the profile simply pre-fills the shared ones.

## Campaign basics

- **Candidate name and office.** Used throughout and in every SEO title.
- **District or jurisdiction (location).** Anchors local copy and the SEO
  keywords.
- **Core message or slogan.** The promise repeated across every page. The spine of
  site consistency.

## Content inputs

- **Top issues.** In order. Populate the home and issues pages.
- **Bio basics.** Background, roots, and why running. Feed the about page.
- **Endorsements list.** Named supporters and any quotes. For the endorsements
  page and credibility elsewhere.
- **Events.** Upcoming event details (what, when, where, RSVP). For the event
  page.
- **Volunteer needs.** The ways supporters can help. For the volunteer page.

## Conversion details

- **Donation link.** The contribution URL the donate page and calls to action
  point to.
- **Required disclaimer text.** The paid-for or authorization line for the donate
  page. The user supplies and confirms it; use a placeholder until then.
- **Contact details.** General and press contact, and social handles.

## Voice and search

- **Tone and brand words.** Professional, populist, or neighborly, plus any words
  the campaign always uses or never uses.
- **Target audience.** Who the site speaks to first.
- **SEO keywords.** The candidate name, office, and location, plus any priority
  search terms. Drives the SEO titles and meta descriptions.
- **Pages to build.** Which pages the user wants. Default to the full set if
  unspecified.

Confirm endorsements, events, and links before publishing. This intake drafts
copy; the campaign verifies the facts.
