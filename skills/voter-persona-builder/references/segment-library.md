# Segment library

Common voter segments to use as starting points. These are hypotheses to adapt to
the campaign's real universe, not prescriptions and not claims about how any
group will vote. Always confirm against the campaign's own voter file, turnout
history, and what organizers hear in the field. Names and concerns below are
prompts to refine, then rename in plain local language.

## By partisanship and support

- **Strong base.** Already with you. Concern is usually turnout, not persuasion.
  Message toward showing up and bringing others.
- **Soft supporters.** Lean your way but not locked in. Concern is doubt or low
  enthusiasm. Message reassures and gives a reason to commit.
- **True persuadables.** Genuinely undecided or split-ticket. Concern is which
  candidate understands their lives. Message contrasts on a top issue, factually.
- **Soft opposition.** Lean the other way but reachable on a specific issue.
  Engage only where there is real common ground.

## By turnout propensity

- **High-propensity base.** Reliable voters who share your side. Focus on
  persuasion of their networks and volunteer recruitment.
- **Low-propensity base.** Support you when they vote, but vote irregularly. The
  whole job is turnout and a concrete plan to vote.
- **New registrants.** Recently registered or recently moved. Concern is often
  not knowing how or where to vote. Lead with the mechanics.

## By life stage and community

- **Young voters.** Often reachable by digital and SMS, motivated by issues that
  shape their future. Avoid talking down to them.
- **Seniors.** Reliable voters who value stability and respect. Often best reached
  by mail, phone, and trusted local messengers.
- **Parents and caregivers.** Time-pressed, motivated by schools, safety, and
  cost of living. Keep asks short and concrete.
- **Working voters under economic strain.** Concern is affordability and respect.
  Populist framing can land if it is specific and not performative.

## By place

- **Rural, suburban, and urban voters** differ in local concerns, trusted
  messengers, and reachable channels. Treat place as a strong segmenting variable
  and let the local issue set define the persona.

## How to use this library

Pick the few segments that this race will actually treat differently, then run
each through the persona structure in SKILL.md. Drop any segment the campaign
cannot reach or would message identically to another. The goal is a short,
usable set, not coverage of every group.
